01 / About me

Where clinical experience
meets AI

I’m Vanessa Vance, an RN with 22 years of pediatric nursing experience. As a bedside nurse, I trained new clinicians to think critically. Today, I train AI to bring a nurse’s careful observation and sound judgment to clinical decisions.

At Optum, I turn clinical policies into decision logic for AI-enabled prior authorization. I was a core contributor to the first UnitedHealth Group team to reach real-time decisioning. My work spans clinical annotation, user acceptance testing, model validation, and post-production enhancements, in partnership with Medical Directors, Engineering, Product, and Business.

I earned my BSN at the University of Pennsylvania. Today, I’m pursuing a graduate certificate in AI in Nursing and Healthcare at The University of Texas at Austin.

Currently learning:

  • Responsible AI in Nursing, FSU/CHAI Microcredential, Fall 2026
  • Favorite podcast: NEJM AI Grand Rounds
02 / Selected work

Making the work
work better.

A closer look at how I contribute across clinical AI, policy, and process. Select a project to read more.

01

Building and testing clinical AI

Turning medical necessity criteria into decision logic for AI-enabled prior authorization.

AI implementation+

My contribution: At Optum, I lead model build timelines and work across the AI development lifecycle - building models, two rounds of user acceptance testing, incorporating feedback, and improving models after launch.

Milestone: Our team was the first UnitedHealth Group team to achieve real-time prior authorization decisioning.

Result: Contributed to workflow automation that shortened the end-to-end AI development lifecycle from four months to 45 days per model.

02

Leading clinical policy implementation

Keeping clinical guidance current across 50 models and 20 health plans.

Clinical informatics+

My contribution: I lead daily CMS guideline updates and monthly policy reviews, coordinating six nurses and three clinical informaticists from review through implementation. This work supports 1,000 model-health plan combinations.

Building team independence: I facilitated recurring office hours with up to four Medical Directors, then trained nursing staff to run the forum independently.

03

Reducing repeat administrative work

A simpler policy-link workflow that saves 12 hours per month.

Process improvement+

My contribution: I identified a shorter policy URL format and developed a simpler linking approach, reducing manual link repairs when CMS policies changed.

Result: Less manual rework and faster policy updates.

Time saved: Six hours per month on daily CMS version updates and another six hours on monthly policy updates.

04

Bringing clinical judgment to AI risk

Identifying a privacy concern and deepening my responsible AI practice.

Responsible AI+

My contribution: I identified a privacy risk in an AI tool, bringing a nursing perspective to the safeguards surrounding its use.

What followed: That work led to six months of mentorship with the Responsible Use for AI Office.
